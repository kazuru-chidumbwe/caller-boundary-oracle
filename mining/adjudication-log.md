# Adjudication log (M1 and M2)

Every disagreement between coder A and coder B is settled by discussion and recorded here (PROTOCOL.md: agreement and adjudication). `mining/kappa.py --log mining/adjudication-log.md` appends one row per disagreement; the last two columns are filled in at the discussion. Definition reviews (a variable below 0.6) are recorded under PROTOCOL.md, "Changes after freezing".
