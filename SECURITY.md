# Security policy

## Issues in this harness

Please report security issues in this repository privately, by email to kazuruuni@gmail.com or through the repository's Security tab when private vulnerability reporting is enabled. Include the commit, the command, and what you expected against what happened. Do not open a public issue for an unfixed vulnerability.

## Findings about third-party libraries

Defects this harness finds in the libraries under test are reported to their maintainers, through their own channels, before they are described in this repository.

## Scope

In scope: drivers, the oracle, chip simulators and the card script, profile and suite generators, scripts and CI. Out of scope: attacks on physical documents, NFC or RF, live PKD infrastructure, and closed-source readers.
